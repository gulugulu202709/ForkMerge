The real-recording pilot snapshot records zero received controlled recordings and no completed two-arm comparison. Upstream example clips do not establish the new replacement/reset paired truth. Real-video forecasts, empirical measurements and semantic learned-memory effects remain missing.

Missing archived assets: complete rendered RGB histories, original train/validation visual feature caches, pretrained encoders and selected/full checkpoints. Recorded hashes are not downloadable files. The replay recomputes evaluation from predictions; it does not regenerate predictions from model weights.

Archive changes are private path/interpreter configuration and explicit UTF-8 text I/O. Numerical routines, constants, seeds, selection settings, stop rule and all96 NPZ bytes are preserved. Newly computed checkpoint/protocol metadata hashes differ after anonymization; every other summary field is checked. Numeric replay tolerances are explicit and do not alter the original experimental thresholds.

`validation/HANDOFF_CHECKS.json` records software/replay verification, not new research efficacy. Native Linux/GPU stages remain untested in this handoff. No GPU/cloud generation is dispatched during release preparation.
